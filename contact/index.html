Before you call

Helpful things to have ready

You don't need everything worked out, but a few details help us understand your project and prepare an accurate quotation.

  1. 1

    The type of work

    Building, roofing, a repair or a renovation? Let us know what you have in mind.

  2. 2

    Where the property is

    Tell us which part of Cape Town the property is in so we can arrange a visit.

  3. 3

    What you've noticed

    For repairs, describe the problem, such as leaks, cracks or damp, and when it started.

  4. 4

    Your timing

    Let us know when you'd like the work done and we'll discuss what's possible.
